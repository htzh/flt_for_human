Lean porting effort

Alongside the prose, this project re-runs selected pieces of the FLT formalisation as our own Lean code. A claim that matters can then be checked rather than taken on faith, and a proof can be restated in a form we find easier to read.

  • 621 Lean modules under lean/FLTForHuman/
  • Lean v4.34.0 · mathlib v4.34.0
  • Fragments only — mathlib is the sole import
  • Every module cites its FLT origin pinned at aa2d8b3

The ground rules: copy small, self-contained definitions and lemmas out of FLT and adapt them — never import the FLT project (one import can pull in a dependency cone and a build measured in hours); adapt rather than mirror, since FLT stays the source of truth for correctness; cite the origin file and commit in each module header; and leave no silent gaps — a module either compiles or carries an explicit, commented sorry.

  • lean/README.md — how the port is organised, pinned, and built.
  • Porting playbook — the reusable method for porting a node, not tied to any one effort.
  • FLTForHuman/ — the verified tree: the ported fragments, by subject.
  • topics/ — work orders and executed plans (PORTING-*.md) for each effort, with what it cost and what it taught.
  • spec/ — the deliverable measures: consumer modules, the statement checker, and the unwired public surface.
  • Carry-forward register — API deliberately left unported, and the open follow-ups.
  • logs/ — the linear record of what happened, in order.

▶ Frontier: level structure and the adelic turn 024–027
  • 027 — The continuity of the idele norm new
    the idele norm, and the norm of the determinant on GL₂ over the adeles, are continuous in the restricted-product topology — the two most heavily depended-on theorems of the pin's NumberField.TateGlobal layer
  • 026 — ℘-division values and the level-N modular curve new
    Fricke functions turn torsion values of ℘ into weight-zero modular functions: SL₂(ℤ) permutes them, the tuple is fixed exactly by ±Γ(N), ℂ(X(N)) = ℂ(j, f_v) is Galois of degree the index, and Shimura's reciprocity law acts on the q-expansions of Δf_v
  • 025 — Elliptic nets, division polynomials, and the invariant of a sequence new
    the classical [n]P formula assembled from three layers — ψ_n as the canonical normalised elliptic divisibility sequence, the elliptic-net relation behind every ψ identity, the complement that avoids ψ₂ₙ/ψ_n, and the invariant 6X²+b₂X+b₄ that removes the division by y, so the construction survives every characteristic
  • 024 — Uniformization and the modular function field: two routes to the j-line new
    the ℚ(j(q), j(qᴺ)) function field and the analytic ℂ/Λ ≅ E(ℂ) theory are complementary, not interchangeable — two independent inputs to the same step of Mazur's irreducibility argument, and the uniformization's surjectivity of j is itself proved by modular forms
