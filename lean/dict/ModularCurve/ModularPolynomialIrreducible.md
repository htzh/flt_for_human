# `ModularCurve/ModularPolynomialIrreducible.lean` — notation dictionary

The module proves that the level-`ℓ` datum is irreducible over `ℚ(j)` (`phiIrreducible_of_splits`)
and symmetric (`evalSymm_of_splits`, `ModularPolynomialData.evalSymm_of_irreducible`), given that its
coefficients split into the conjugate product. Almost all of its notation exists to keep the
**three images of one formal polynomial** apart; that is what this file fixes.

Module: [ModularPolynomialIrreducible.lean](../../FLTForHuman/ModularCurve/ModularPolynomialIrreducible.lean)

| `jAdj`, `evalAtJAdj` | `Defs/PhiGen` | `j(q)` inside `ℚ[j(q)]`, and `ℤ[X] →+* ℚ[j(q)]`, `X ↦ j(q)` |

Two maps are private and exist only in this module:

| name | type | action |
|---|---|---|
| `qEmbedT K ℓ` | `ℚ((q)) →+* K((q))` | `q ↦ q ^ ℓ`, then `ℚ → K`: `j(q) ↦ j(q ^ ℓ)` |
| `adjoinEmbedT K ℓ` | **`ℚ[j(q)] →+* K((q))`** | the same substitution, but on the coefficient ring `ℚ[j(q)]` |

## 2. The datum

`data : ModularPolynomialData ℓ` is `Φ ∈ ℤ[X][Y]`, monic of degree `ψ(ℓ) = ℓ + 1`, vanishing at
`(j(q), j(q ^ ℓ))`. It is the level-`ℓ` modular polynomial with the three facts the cone needs, and
it is unique at a prime (`ModularPolynomialData.eq_of_prime`), so "the datum at `ℓ`" is a definite
object. Read `data.Φ` as that polynomial; the other fields are its certificate.

What the datum does **not** carry is the splitting of the fibre at `X = j(q ^ ℓ)`. That is a separate
hypothesis (`hsplit`) of the theorems that need it, proved elsewhere from the descended coefficient
family; it is not a consequence of the three facts.

## 3. Levels — the objects

| name | definition | coefficient ring | `Y` |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Φ` | `data.Φ` | `ℤ` | formal |
| `Φ₀` | `data.Φ.map evalAtJAdj`, i.e. `Φ(j(q), Y)` | `ℚ[j(q)]` | formal |
| `Φ₁` | `Φ₀.map (adjoinEmbedT K ℓ)`, i.e. `Φ(j(q ^ ℓ), Y)` | `K((q))` | formal |
| `data.toAdjoin` | `data.Φ.map evalAtJGen`, i.e. `Φ(j(q), Y)` | `ℚ(j(q))` | formal |
| `A`, `B` | the two factors in a factorisation of `Φ₀` | `ℚ[j(q)]` | formal |
| `A ^ σ`, `B ^ σ` | informal for `A.map (adjoinEmbedT K ℓ)` and the same for `B` | `K((q))` | formal |

`Φ₀` and `Φ₁` are introduced by `set` at the top of `phiIrreducible_of_splits`. Irreducibility is
decided at `Φ₀`: `PhiIrreducible data` is `Irreducible data.toAdjoin`, and Gauss
(`phiIrreducible_iff_irreducible_map_evalAtJAdj`) moves it to `Φ₀` across `ℚ[j(q)] ⊂ ℚ(j(q))`.

## 4. Points — the `Y`-variable

| point | in | role |
|---|---|---|
| `coeffEmb K jq` | `K((q))` | `j(q)`, which is `conj ℓ ζ (0 : Fin ℓ).succ`; the point at which the conjugate product vanishes, hence the factor the root count kills |
| `conj ℓ ζ i` | `K((q))` | the `ℓ + 1` roots of `Φ₁`; the `ℓ` finite ones are all hit from one, by twist invariance of the coefficients |
| `β : adjoinJq` | `ℚ[j(q)]` | the only place `Y` is evaluated in `ℚ[j(q)]`: `hnoRoot` rules out roots there |

## 5. Three spellings of one polynomial

The level-`ℓ` image `Φ₁` is written three ways, and recognising them is most of the reading:

- `Φ₀.map (adjoinEmbedT K ℓ)` — the level change on the coefficient ring;
- `data.Φ.map ((qEmbedT K ℓ).comp evalAtJ)` — the same map, `X` slot first;
- `data.Φ.map (((coeffEmb K).comp (qExpand ℚ ℓ)).comp evalAtJ)` — the pin's spelling, used in the
  hypothesis `hsplit`; `coeffEmb_qExpand` says it is the same composite.

## 6. The centre of the proof: `hnoRoot`

Everything else in `phiIrreducible_of_splits` is degree bookkeeping; the content is
`hnoRoot : ∀ β : adjoinJq, Φ₀.eval β ≠ 0`, that `Φ(j(q), Y)` has no root in `ℚ[j(q)]`.

Step 1 — the level change, from a root in `ℚ[j(q)]` to a root in `K((q))`. This is a congruence,
not a computation: a coefficient ring hom takes a root to a root, which is mathlib's
`Polynomial.IsRoot.map` (`(Φ₀.map σ).eval (σ β) = σ (Φ₀.eval β)`), so with `σ = adjoinEmbedT K ℓ`
and `Φ₁ = Φ₀.map σ` the vanishing at `β` gives one at `σ β`:

```lean
    rw [← hsplit₁, hΦ₁]
    exact Polynomial.IsRoot.map hβ
```

Hence `σ β` is a root of the conjugate product, and a product of linear factors vanishes only if a
factor does — `exists_eq_conj_of_eval_phiProd_eq_zero`, the one root-counting step of the proof:
`∃ i, σ β = conj ℓ ζ i`.

Step 2 — the two cases of `Fin (ℓ + 1)`:

- **the extra conjugate** (`i = 0`, so `σ β = conj 0 = j(q ^ (ℓ * ℓ))`). This is where the
  arithmetic input enters, and it is one named descent:
  `eq_jqN_of_adjoinEmbedT_eq_conj_zero` — a `calc` through `qEmbedT = adjoinEmbedT = conj 0 =
  qEmbedT (jqN ℓ)`, so injectivity of `qEmbedT` gives `β = j(q ^ ℓ)`. Then `j(q ^ ℓ) ∈ ℚ[j(q)]`,
  which `jqN_not_mem_adjoin_jq` rules out — the positivity of `j`'s coefficients at `n = ℓ` inside
  a twist-sum coefficient. This case is where the module's one arithmetic input, `one_le_coeff_jq`,
  is consumed; the argument is §11.
- **a finite conjugate** (`i = b.succ`, so `σ β = j(ζ ^ b * q)`). No non-membership is needed; the
  two sides are separated by their `q⁻¹` coefficient:
  - `j(q) = q⁻¹ + O(1)`, and the nome twist `q ↦ ζ ^ b q` multiplies the `q⁻¹` coefficient by
    `ζ ^ (-b)`, so *every* finite conjugate has coefficient `(ζ ^ b)⁻¹ ≠ 0` at `-1`
    (`conj_succ_coeff_neg_one`);
  - the level-`ℓ` substitution moves the pole from `q⁻¹` to `q ^ (-ℓ)`, so a level-`ℓ` image is a
    series in `q ^ ℓ` and has coefficient `0` at `-1` (`ℓ ∤ -1`, `coeff_neg_one_adjoinEmbedT`).

  `-1` is the slot where the pole of `j` of order `1` sits: it is nonzero after every twist and
  empty after `q ↦ q ^ ℓ`, so it separates the two kinds of conjugate.

## 7. The root count: `le_natDegree_of_eval_map_jqK_eq_zero`

"If `D ≠ 0` over `ℚ[j(q)]` and its level-`ℓ` image vanishes at `j(q)`, then `ℓ ≤ D.natDegree`." Read
off three named facts:

| name | statement |
|---|---|
| `hconj` | the `ℓ` finite conjugates `b ↦ conj b.succ` are distinct (`conj_injective` + `Fin.succ_injective`) |
| `hroots` | the level-`ℓ` image vanishes at every one of them (`eval_map_conj_succ_eq_zero`: `ζ^b` fixes `ℚ[j(q)]`'s image, since the image is a series in `q ^ ℓ`) |
| `hDσ` | the image is nonzero (`adjoinEmbedT_injective`) |

then `deg D ^ σ ≥ ℓ` by the root bound, and `deg D ^ σ ≤ deg D` (`natDegree_map_le`) gives the
claim; the closing `calc` is exactly these two inequalities.

## 8. Names inside `phiIrreducible_of_splits`

| name | statement |
|---|---|
| `hsplit` | hypothesis: `Φ₁` is the conjugate product, in the pin's spelling |
| `hsplit₁` | `Φ₁ = phiProd ℓ (conj ℓ ζ)`, via the intermediate `data.Φ.map ((qEmbedT K ℓ).comp evalAtJ)` |
| `hmonic`, `hdeg` | `Φ₀.Monic`, and `Φ₀.natDegree = ℓ + 1` (`natDegree Φ = ψ(ℓ) = ℓ + 1`) |
| `hnoRoot` | see §6 |
| `hAB` | `Φ₀ = A * B` |
| `hA0`, `hB0` | `A ≠ 0`, `B ≠ 0` |
| `hlc`, `hlcA`, `hlcB` | `A.leadingCoeff * B.leadingCoeff = 1`, and the two unit facts |
| `hsum` | `A.natDegree + B.natDegree = ℓ + 1` |
| `hAdvd`, `hBdvd` | `A ∣ Φ₀`, `B ∣ Φ₀` |
| `hzero` | `(A ^ σ).eval (coeffEmb K jq) * (B ^ σ).eval (coeffEmb K jq) = 0`, through `Φ₁` |
| `hAdeg`, `hBdeg` | `ℓ ≤ A.natDegree` (resp. `B`), from `le_natDegree_of_eval_map_jqK_eq_zero` |
| `hB1`, `hA1` | the surviving factor has degree `≤ 1`, so it is a unit by `isUnit_of_dvd_of_natDegree_le_one` |

The one generic step of the proof is `isUnit_of_dvd_of_natDegree_le_one`: a divisor of degree `≤ 1`
with unit leading coefficient of a polynomial with no root is a unit. It mentions no datum and no
level; its two cases are degree `0` (unit constant) and degree `1` (would have the root
`-u⁻¹ * p.coeff 0`).

## 10. The same fact elsewhere in the port

`j(q ^ p) ∉ ℚ(j(q))` is developed more than once, at different levels, and the developments are
*nested*, not parallel:

| statement | module | proof |
|---|---|---|
| `jqN_not_mem_adjoin_jq` (private, §6): `jqN p ∉ ℚ[j(q)]` | here | the `q ^ (-p)` coefficient of the twist sum, against the positivity of `j`'s coefficients at `n = p` |
| `jqN_prime_not_mem_base` (private): `jqN r ∉ ℚ⟮jq⟯` | `FunctionFieldGeneration/Nonmembership.lean` | `finrank_adjoin_jqN_eq_of_prime r`: the relative degree is `r + 1 > 1`, so no field element is `j(q ^ r)` |
| `prime_notMem_levelField`: `levelSeries jg p ∉ levelField jg M` for `p ∤ M`, over a general coefficient field | `FunctionFieldGeneration/Generic/PrimeNonmembership.lean` | that degree fact as the base case (`hbase`), with the slot product and the `Φ_p` dichotomy for the level step |

The first and second are the polynomial-ring and the field-level forms of one statement — the field
one is the stronger conclusion — and the order between them is forced by the imports:
`Nonmembership.lean` imports `ModularPolynomialProperties`, hence this module, and the finrank
theorem it spends comes from `exists_phiIrreducible_evalSymm`, i.e. from `phiIrreducible_of_splits`
and so from `hnoRoot`. The coefficient-level refutation proved here is therefore the input of the
degree-level one; it cannot be replaced by it, and deleting it would break the chain.

## 11. The twist-sum refutation (the non-membership)

`jqN_not_mem_adjoin_jq` says `j(q ^ ℓ)` is not a polynomial in `j(q)`; it is the module's one genuine
argument besides the factor count. Suppose `P(j(q)) = j(q ^ ℓ)` with `P ∈ ℚ[X]`.

1. **`P` is monic of degree `ℓ`.** `j(q) = q⁻¹ + O(1)`, so `P(j(q))` has pole order exactly `deg P`
   with leading coefficient `lc P` (`poleOrderLE_aeval_jq`, `coeff_aeval_jq_neg`), while
   `j(q ^ ℓ) = q ^ (-ℓ) + O(1)`. Comparing the lowest exponents gives `deg P = ℓ` and `lc P = 1`
   (`natDegree_eq_of_aeval_jq_eq_jqN`, `monic_of_aeval_jq_eq_jqN`).
2. **Base change.** Read the identity over a field with a primitive `ℓ`-th root `ζ` — the cyclotomic
   field, coefficients along `ratC` (`eval₂_ratC_jqK_of_aeval_jq_eq`).
3. **Twists.** The nome action `τ_b : q ↦ ζ ^ b q` fixes `ℚ` and fixes `j(q ^ ℓ)` (because
   `ζ ^ (b * ℓ) = 1`), and sends `j(q)` to `j(ζ ^ b q)`. So each of the `ℓ` twists of `j(q)` is a
   root of `P(Y) = j(q ^ ℓ)` (`eval₂_ratC_conj_succ_of_aeval_jq_eq`).
4. **Vieta.** Those `ℓ` points are distinct, and `P(Y) - j(q ^ ℓ)` is monic of degree `ℓ`, so it
   equals `∏_b (Y - j(ζ ^ b q))`; comparing the coefficient of `Y ^ (ℓ - 1)` gives
   `∑_b j(ζ ^ b q) = -P.coeff (ℓ - 1)`, a **rational number**
   (`sum_conj_succ_eq_ratC_of_aeval_jq_eq`; its proof is the standard one: two monic polynomials of
   degree `ℓ` agreeing at `ℓ` distinct points differ by a constant, so their `Y ^ (ℓ - 1)`
   coefficients agree).
5. **The coefficient at `q ^ ℓ`.** A twist multiplies the coefficient at exponent `m` by
   `ζ ^ (b * m)`, and `ζ ^ (b * ℓ) = 1`, so all `ℓ` twists share the coefficient at `ℓ`, namely
   `jq.coeff ℓ`; the sum's coefficient there is `ℓ * jq.coeff ℓ ≠ 0` — `ℓ ≠ 0` in the field, and
   positivity `jq.coeff ℓ ≥ 1` (`coeff_sum_conj_succ_self`, `coeff_sum_conj_succ_ne_zero`) —
   whereas a rational constant has coefficient `0` at a positive exponent (`coeff_ratC_of_ne`).

The exponent used is `ℓ`, not `-ℓ`: the positive multiples of `ℓ` are exactly the exponents that all
`ℓ` twists fix, and `j`'s coefficients there are nonvanishing and positive, while its pole sits at
`-1` (which is what step 1 reads). This is the only place `one_le_coeff_jq` is consumed in the
module.
