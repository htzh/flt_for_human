## 1. What the set says

- **A** — a subgroup `Γ₁(M) ≤ Γ ≤ Γ₀(M)` is `Γ_H(M)` for its image `H` in `(ZMod M)ˣ`.
- **B** — `[Γ₀(M) : Γ_H(M)] = [Γ₀(M) : H]`, through the surjectivity of `gamma0Units`.
- **C** — a lift of a unit of `ZMod M` to `Γ₀(M)` with lower-left entry divisible by a prime.
- **D** — `H¹(Γ_H(M), A)` is finite over the coefficient ring (`IharaSubgroup`: the `Γ_H` subgroup is a
  congruence subgroup, `evalAt` on a generating set is injective, and the module is Noetherian).
- **E** — the character involution of the cohomology commutes with every operator of the Hecke family.
- **F** — the double cosets of `Γ_H(M)` are generated by the powers of one element, of additive order
  the order of the corresponding unit (`GammaHDoubleCoset`).
- **G** — the weight-two Eichler--Shimura map: a `Γ_H(M)`-invariant `J`-equivariant period map from
  cusp-form pairs to `H¹`, injective, with range exactly the parabolic part, and Hecke-equivariant.
- **H** — the parabolic cohomology has a two-element eigenbasis, and every character's simultaneous
  eigenspace is two-dimensional.
