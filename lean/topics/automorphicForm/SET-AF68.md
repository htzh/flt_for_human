## 1. What the set says

- **A** — *the Atkin--Lehner--Li basis of `S_k(Γ₁(M), ε)`*: finitely many pairwise distinct primitive
  forms `g_i` of levels `L_i ∣ M` with `changeLevel (ε_i) = ε`, together with the degeneracy images
  `G_{i,d}` over `d ∣ M / L_i`, are linearly independent and span the `ε`-nebentypus part of the
  weight-`k` cusp forms. The engine is the `PF` package (level, character, coefficients) and the
  coefficient linear functional `qCoeffLin` that separates the degeneracies.
- **B** — *the same basis for `Γ_H(M)`*, with the classical action computed: `T_ℓ` (`ℓ ∤ M`) acts by
  `a_ℓ(g_i)`, `⟨u⟩` by the character value at level `M` (and `ε_i(u) = 1` for `u ∈ H`), and `U_q`
  (`q ∣ M`) by the three-branch recursion (`q ∣ d` lowers `d`, `q ∣ L_i` gives `a_q`, neither gives
  `a_q G_{i,d} − ε_i(q) q^{k−1} G_{i,dq}`).
- **C** — *the commutation of the five operator pairs* on `Γ_H(M)`-cusp forms: `T_ℓ` with `T_ℓ'`,
  `T_ℓ` with `U_q`, `T_ℓ` with the diamonds, `U_q` with the diamonds, and the diamonds with each other.
  The proof diagonalises all three families on the B basis (`comm_of_scalar_of_stab`).
- **D** — *strong multiplicity one in weight two*: for a finite set `S` of primes, the `ℂ`-algebra
  generated by the `T_ℓ` with `ℓ ∉ S`, the `U_q` and the diamonds on the weight-two `Γ_H(M)` cusp-form
  space has a one-vector eigenbasis, and every character's simultaneous eigenspace is
  one-dimensional. The engine is the weight-two copy of the B datum plus the `U`-divisor-string
  endomorphism `Ur` (`ev_linearIndependent`, `ev_span`, `Ur_comm`, `Ur_down`, `Ur_base`, `string`,
  `lift`, `cyclic`, `eigen_le_one`).
