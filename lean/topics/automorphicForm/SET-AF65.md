## 1. What the set says

- **A** — a `Γ₁(N)`-cusp form with nebentypus `ε` whose coefficients vanish at the indices prime to `K`
  is a sum `∑ p ∣ N, (V_p g_p)` of degeneracy lifts with the level-changed nebentypus. The proof reads
  AF63's level-lowering decomposition, then uses AF64's `HasNebentypus.sum_filter_eq_of_sum_eq` to
  separate the nebentypus pieces and AF64's eigenbasis to read each piece's eigenvalues.
- **B** — the same form is `∑_i c i • (g i ∣ V_{d i})` with the `g i` *primitive* at the levels
  `M i ∣ N` and `d i * M i ∣ N`: the newform decomposition. Its prelude is the `ALLiDecomposition`
  block (the `PrimData` package, the `Forms`/`Prim`/`Induction` sections).
- **C** — multiplicity one: two Hecke eigenforms of the same level, weight and eigenvalues are
  proportional. The proof is the `PerPrime`/`Main` sections plus the `q`-coefficient comparison
  (`UpperHalfPlane.eq_of_forall_qCoeff_eq`).
- **D** — the `ℤ`-algebra generated by a weight-two eigenform's `q`-coefficients and its nebentypus
  values on `(ZMod M)ˣ` is a finitely generated `ℤ`-module, from the integral basis of the weight-two
  `Γ₁` cusp space. **E** is the same statement in `Module.Finite ℤ` form, one line.
- **F, G** — `Γ₁(M) ≤ Γ_H(M)` and back: a `Γ_H`-form is a `Γ₁`-form, and a nebentypus form trivial on
  `H` is a `Γ_H`-form. **H** — the underlying functions of the `Γ_H` Hecke operators. **I** — the
  `Γ_H`-nebentypus decomposition of the cusp space and the independence of its components.
