Rules that apply to all four:

* **Drop only the pin's `namespace P2MW.S_<stem>` bookkeeping prefix.** Keep the pin's inner
  namespaces exactly (`TrFrob`/`TrDesc`, `Matrix.SU2EulerAux`, `Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup.BruhatGeneration`,
  `Matrix`, and row 4's `S06W2` block): the checker diffs statement *text*, and a statement that
  mentions a sibling by its short name stays hash-identical only if the declarations keep their
  namespace. Do not "improve" the namespaces — a rename is a future revision, not this set.
* **Header:** a leading `/- … -/` block with the module's subject, the pin file and its line count,
  what it assumes, and then, **`Theorems/` wrapper line first**:
  ```lean
  spec-pin: Theorems/Thm_<stem>.lean
  spec-pin: P2M/Sol/S_<stem>.lean
  ```
  (`stem` = the node's name with `.` → `_`). A listed `S_` file shadows its own wrapper otherwise.
* **The headline is the wrapper's statement, verbatim** — its text, not the `S_` file's. This
  matters for row 4, where the two differ (`Matrix.SpecialLinearGroup` vs `SpecialLinearGroup`,
  `port_advise` ratio 0.982): take the wrapper's.
* **`solution` is not ported** (all four files have one). State the headline once, at the wrapper's
  name, with the pin's proof body.
* **Private stays private.** Row 1 has one `private` helper (the 89-line `descent`), row 3 has
  **eight** (`dk`, `nk`, `wk`, `genSet`, …) and its whole proof is private — so row 3 wires exactly
  **one** declaration. Do not promote any of them (playbook §4b: a helper is promoted when another
  theory states it, and nothing does).
* Row 2's `main` and row 4's `S06W2.exists_specialLinearGroup_mul_upperTriangular` are the pin's
  proof-bearing publics: keep them public at the pin's names, since they are part of the verified
  surface.
