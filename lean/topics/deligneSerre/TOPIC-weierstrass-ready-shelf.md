## 2. The four stories

### A — exceptional automorphisms of the supersingular curves (1,412 lines)

* `…_of_char_two` (988, Silverman III.10.1 + Appendix A Prop. 1.2): on
  `y² + y = x³` over an algebraically closed `L` of characteristic 2, package the order-three
  and order-four automorphisms `ω`, `i` as **additive endomorphisms** `σ`, `ι` of
  `E₀(L)`. The content is the realization half: `σ` is the variable-change action of
  `(u,r,s,t) = (w,0,0,0)` with `w³ = 1`, `ι` that of `(1,1,1,w)`, recorded through
  `Point.vcInvFun` as a heterogeneous equality; then `σ² + σ + 1 = 0`, `ι² = -1`, and neither
  is scalar on any `p`-torsion for `p ∣ M`.
* `…_of_char_three` (424, Deuring; Husemöller Ch. 3): on `y² = x³ − x` in characteristic 3
  the automorphism group is generated by `α` (from `i`, `i² = −1`) and the characteristic-3-only
  order-three map `(x,y) ↦ (x−1,y)`; the statement is a **classification** — the six maps
  `id, α, β, β², αβ, αβ²` modulo `±1` are exactly the variable changes fixing `E₀`.

Payoff: the order-three torsion-basis node needs **both** (3,125-line demand), the order-four
basis one uses char 2 (2,193), the char-2 `Γ_H(M)` orbit/double-coset count one uses char 2
(2,246), and Igusa's char-3 supersingular census uses char 3 — a **96,838-line** demand. The
other consumers of these two are out of the Deligne–Serre cone.

### B — full level structure and division fields (910 lines)

* `nonempty_torsionBy_…_of_natCard_torsion_eq_sq` (68): if `Ω/k` already carries all `N²`
  points of `E[N]` with `N` invertible, then `E(Ω)[N] ≅ (ℤ/N)²`. Proof: pass to `Ω̄`, where
  the ported alg-closed isomorphism counts, and injectivity of the extension map on
  `N`-torsion forces the same cardinality.
* `exists_intermediateField_isGalois_card_torsion_eq_sq` (842): existence of the `n`-division
  field `F(E[n])` inside a fixed algebraic closure — finite over `F`, Galois, exactly `n²`
  points, Galois action on `E[n]` **faithful**. Self-contained: separable-closure helpers and
  a `sepPoints` subgroup of the base change, resting on one ported premise.

Payoff: the moduli layer — `exists_equiv_algHom_qExpFunctionFieldC_gammaH_of_transcendental_j`
(85,536), `exists_natural_diamond_algHom_…` (85,755), `exists_isGalois_ord_dvd_six_…
_of_char_three` (76,856) — plus the good-Deuring-model node (1,627). The division-field node
has project fan-in 11 and also pays into the Čerednik–Drinfeld and projective-model columns.

### C — the Serre–Tate / good-reduction mechanism (786 lines, gated — see §3)

* `exists_reduceHom_eq_of_nsmul_eq_zero_of_natCast_ne_zero` (185, Silverman VII.3.1): over a
  valuation subring with `Δ(W mod 𝔪) ≠ 0`, reduction is **surjective** on `ℓ`-torsion for any
  `ℓ` invertible in the residue field. This is the classical "reduction is an isomorphism on
  prime-to-`p` torsion" half.
* `exists_inertia_equivariant_reduction_of_variableChange_eq_map` (601, Serre–Tate 1968;
  Silverman VII.1.3, VII.2.1, VII.3.1): an additive `θ` on points plus, for each inertia
  element `σ`, a variable change `g_σ` over `A` with `ḡ_σ • W̃ = W̃`,
  `θ(σP) = vcInvFun ḡ_σ (θ P)`, and `ḡ_{στ} = ḡ_σ ḡ_τ`; injective on the prime-to-`p` part.

### D — Vélu quotients and the `j`-map on cyclic subgroups (760 lines)

* `stepCurve_stepSubgroup_eq_of_prime_ne_two` (235): the odd prime-degree step of the
  abscissa-indexed iterated Vélu construction at `(E,H)` equals Vélu's quotient by `⟨Q⟩`
  (`Q ∈ H`, `addOrderOf Q = ℓ`), with accompanying subgroup `φ(H)` — the identification that
  makes `stepCurve` and `veluQuotient` interchangeable.
* `zmultiples_eq_of_veluQuotient_j_eq_of_forall_isogenyEndDatum_exists_int` (525): over the
  Hahn-series curve with `Hom(E,E) ≅ ℤ` (Silverman III.4.12 through `IsogenyEndDatum`),
  equal nonzero discriminants and equal `j`-invariants of two odd Vélu quotients force
  `ℤQ = ℤQ'`.

Payoff: `bijOn_cyclicQuotientJ_isRoot_modularPolynomial_of_transcendental_j` (23,688),
`zmultiples_…_of_transcendental` (3,368), and above them the 24k/36k/60k
`exists_algEquiv_…` nodes.

### 5.1 Story D splits: `zmultiples` is blocked by a co-import collision

Scoping W3 turned up a hard collision. `WeierstrassCurve.zmultiples_eq_of_veluQuotient_j_eq_of_forall_isogenyEndDatum_exists_int`
has premises on both sides of a pair of modules that **cannot be imported together**:

* `WeierstrassCurve.exists_veluFunctionFieldHom_pointMapOfPushforward_ker_eq_zmultiples` is
  declared **only** in `FLTForHuman/WeierstrassCurve/Velu/PointMapOddOrder.lean`, which
  directly imports `Velu/RestrictAlong.lean`; and its header records that both of that
  module's headlines are built on RestrictAlong's `exists_veluFunctionFieldHom_restrictAlong_placeOfPoint_eq`,
  `pointMapOfPushforward_eq_of_seam_cf`, the three `_cf` helpers and
  `normFormulaAlong_of_separableAlong` — the import is load-bearing, not vestigial.
* `exists_pointEnd_eq_of_mem_isogenyEndSubring`, `exists_zsmul_eq_of_isAlgClosed`,
  `card_torsionBy_eq_sq_of_isAlgClosed` and `exists_pointHom_comp_eq_of_ker_le_of_isCentred`
  each have their port homes in `IsogenyEndDatum/{CharPolySquare,DualEndData,PointEndSubring,Vocabulary}.lean`
  or `Isogeny/KernelBaseChange.lean`, and **every one of those reaches
  `IsogenyEndDatum/Engine.lean`** (measured: 104, 100, 104, 102 and 114 modules of cone).

And `IsogenyEndDatum/Engine.lean` + `Velu/RestrictAlong.lean` are unimportable together —
verified, not inferred:

```text
error: import …Velu.RestrictAlong failed, environment already contains
  'WeierstrassCurve.Affine.normFormulaAlong_of_elliptic' from …IsogenyEndDatum.Engine
```

The clash is the one [`CARRY-FORWARD.md`](../../CARRY-FORWARD.md) already registers: the
special char-zero copy of `normFormulaAlong_of_elliptic` in `Engine.lean:152` and the general
char-free copy in `Velu/RestrictAlong.lean:1843`. `instInfinitePlace` no longer collides
(`Engine.lean:96` is now `private`, `RRSpace.lean:444` `scoped`), so the *only* remaining
breakage is this one declaration.

**Two routes, both the manager's, neither taken yet.**

1. **Unblock by refactor (recommended).** CARRY-FORWARD already prescribes it: keep one public
   `normFormulaAlong_of_elliptic` (the general copy is the H4 statement; the H5 copy has six
   internal callers in `Engine`/`DualEndData`), rename the other, and move the affected
   `spec/` consumer zones with it — the checker matches by last name, so a rename must be
   mirrored there. This is a frozen-module wave: price it with
   `tools/deps/build_ladder.py --edit` for both hosts before touching anything. It unblocks
   not just `zmultiples` but any future module that needs both cones.
2. **Park the node.** Leave `zmultiples` on the shelf and note that its two halves are already
   paid for on both sides; nothing else on this shelf needs the pair.

**Resolved 2026-10-08 by route 1, and it was cheaper than the entry feared.** Measuring the
blast radius first showed the two copies are *not* symmetric: every reference outside
`Velu/RestrictAlong.lean` itself lives in a module whose cone contains `Engine` (checked per

Velu.RestrictAlong` now elaborates, as does the whole `zmultiples` premise union. Checker
7234 → 7233 identical / 37 own (the one declaration moves from the verified public surface to
