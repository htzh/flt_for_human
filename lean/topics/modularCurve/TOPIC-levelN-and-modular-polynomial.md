## 1. Subject A — the level-`N` function field

**The mathematics.** `ModularCurve.LevelN.ring N` is the `ℂ`-subalgebra of `ℍ → ℂ` generated by
`jAnalytic` and the Fricke functions `fricke N v = -(E₄ * E₆ / discriminant) / 2592 * wp N v`,
where `wp N v τ` is the Weierstrass `℘`-function of the period pair `PeriodPair.ofTau τ` at the
`N`-torsion point `(v 0 * τ + v 1)/N`. The nodes prove the classical description of the field of
modular functions of level `N`: its fraction field `K` is Galois over `ℂ(jGen)`, the Galois group
is `SL(2, ℤ)/±Γ(N)`, and the places of `K` are the cusps and the points above each `τ₀`, with the
q-expansion at `∞` and the evaluation at `τ₀` realized as places and the analytic order at `τ₀`
equal to `e` times the place's `ord`.

| node (`ModularCurve.` prefix) | `S_` lines | content |
|---|---:|---|
| `LevelN.exists_monoidHom_algEquiv_fixedField_eq_adjoin` | 472 | the `SL(2, ℤ)`-action on `K`: kernel `Γ(N) ⊔ ±1`, fixed field `ℂ(jGen)`, `[K : ℂ(jGen)] = index`, `IsGalois`, `jGen` transcendental |
| `LevelN.exists_algHom_laurentSeries_qExpansion` | 602 | the q-expansion at `∞` is a `ℂ`-place `K →ₐ[ℂ] LaurentSeries ℂ`, sending `jGen` to the q-expansion of `j` and commuting with q-expansions |
| `LevelN.exists_place_ord_neg_forall_smul_eq` | 891 | a cusp place: `ord jGen < 0`, fixed by the automorphisms fixing the `T`-cusp |

## 2. Subject B — the modular polynomial `Φ_N`

**The mathematics.** `ModularPolynomialData N` packages the modular polynomial `Φ_N ∈ ℤ[X, Y]`
and its `toAdjoin`. The nodes complete the classical theory on top of the already-ported
`Φ` machinery: the diagonal leading coefficient is a unit when `N` is not a square; `Φ_N` is
irreducible modulo a prime `p` (in the sense used by the column); its symmetry; the uniqueness
row `aeval (jqN N) data.toAdjoin = 0`; the star/Atkin–Lehner action on its roots; and the `N = 2`
fibre computed through the Vélu quotient `W/⟨P⟩`.
