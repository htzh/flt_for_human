# The degeneracy-cover degree: `relIndex` against `relrank`

**Status: scoping + ground landed, bodies dispatched (2026-10-21).** This note is the planning
record for the pair of `ModularCurve` nodes that compute the degree of the degeneracy covering

```
X(Γ ∩ Γ₀(N))  ⟶  X(Γ)
```

from the two sides: the relative index of the congruence subgroup, and the relative degree of
the `q`-expansion function field generated by $`j(q^N)`$. The two theorems are the two
inequalities of one equality, they share about a third of their pin lines, and they were
therefore taken as **one set with one frozen ground**.

Order: [WORKORDER-RI1-relindex-relrank-pair.md](WORKORDER-RI1-relindex-relrank-pair.md).
The methods note this round follows is
[../TOPIC-dedup-refactoring.md](../TOPIC-dedup-refactoring.md) (§3 the four tests, §4 the
checker's orientation, §5 the procedure, §6 what not to do).

Pin `aa2d8b3`:
[the `RelIndex` file](https://github.com/anthropics/fermats-last-theorem/blob/aa2d8b3/P2M/Sol/S_ModularCurve_relIndex_gamma0_le_relrank_adjoin_insert_jqNModC.lean),
[the `RelrankNorm` file](https://github.com/anthropics/fermats-last-theorem/blob/aa2d8b3/P2M/Sol/S_ModularCurve_relrank_adjoin_qExpansion_div_le_relIndex.lean).

For $`\Gamma \le \mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb{Z})`$ with $`T \in \Gamma`$, $`N \neq 0`$, and
$`A = A(\Gamma)`$ the field of ratios of $`q`$-expansions of forms on $`\Gamma`$:

* **`LeRelrank`** (the 1,214-line node): $`[\,\Gamma : \Gamma \cap \Gamma_0(N)\,] \le
  [\,A(j(q^N)) : A\,]`$ — "enough conjugates". The proof exhibits $`\mathrm{relIndex}`$-many
  distinct series $`\rho(\gamma)`$, the $`N^2`$-stretched twists of $`j`$ indexed by the
  $`\Gamma_0(N)`$-cosets of $`\Gamma`$, as roots of a polynomial of degree
  $`\lt \mathrm{relIndex}`$; the cosets are parametrised by the Hermite normal form of
  $`\gamma`$, and the injectivity $`\rho(\gamma_1) = \rho(\gamma_2) \Rightarrow \gamma_2\gamma_1^{-1}
  \in \Gamma_0(N)`$ is read off the coefficient of $`q^{-1}`$.
* **`LeRelIndex`** (the 545-line node): $`[\,A(\Gamma') : A(\Gamma)\,] \le [\,\Gamma : \Gamma'\,]`$
  for $`\Gamma' \le \Gamma`$ — the norm argument. The proof takes the characteristic polynomial
  of $`c \mapsto`$ "multiplication by $`c g - f`$" under the norm from $`\Gamma'`$ to
  $`\Gamma`$, interpolates its coefficients as modular forms on $`\Gamma`$
  (`coeffForm`), and concludes by bounded degree.

Together they give $`[A(\Gamma \cap \Gamma_0(N)) : A(\Gamma)] = [\Gamma : \Gamma \cap \Gamma_0(N)]`$,
which is what the Hecke-degeneracy degree (`finrankAlong_heckeAlphaHBar_of_dvd`, the
`LeRelrank` node's only in-cone consumer) consumes.

Two decisions are worth recording because they were taken against a tool reading:

* **`DiamondLift.IsRatio` was not imported.** `port_advise` reported `qC_add`/`qC_mul`/`qC_neg`
  against `XH/DiamondLiftPrelude.lean` and would have reported the ratio field too, but
  `IsRatio`/`ratioField` carry `[IsLevel Γ]` **and** `[Γ.FiniteIndex]`, and neither theorem of
  this set assumes `Γ` has finite index (it is an arbitrary subgroup containing `T`). The
  host is in the wrong generality for the consumer, so §3.2 applies: the definition is stated
  once without instances. Unifying the two is a separate order.
* **the two spellings were collapsed.** The bodies' `S`/`SField` are *not* reproduced; both
  bodies use `ratioSet`/`ratioField`. §3.1's warning (two `IsRat`s forty lines apart) is the
  reason: the pin's duplication is not information to be preserved.
