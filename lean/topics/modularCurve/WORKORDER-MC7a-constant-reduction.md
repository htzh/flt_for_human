## 1. What the mathematics is

`h : ReductionInputsQExpModL A Γ` gives a place reduction (`IsLaurentPlaceReduction`) for
`FF = laurentBaseChange (AlgebraicClosure ℚ) (qExpFunctionFieldC ℚ Γ)` and the fact that the
principal divisors of `FF` are generated by integral ones. The theorem constructs

```lean
R : ConstantReduction A FF Fb        -- Fb = qExpFunctionFieldC (IsLocalRing.ResidueField A) Γ
```

with `R.placeMap = placeReductionQExpModL h`, `R.pic0Map = reductionQExpModL A Γ`, and residue
`coeffMap (IsLocalRing.residue A)`. The residue ring is the Gauss ring `{f | IsInt A f}`
(`∃ x y, ϖ y ≠ 0 ∧ f * ι y = ι x`); the residue map is `resF`; the hard step is that its image is
*the* function field `Fb` (`resF_surjective`) and that the place map extends to divisors
(`mapDomain_placeMap`, by the closure induction and the "ord-0 element is an `A`-constant" step).

Pin: the `PlaceMap` section, `S_:788–930` (~145 lines) is the new content; the headline wrapper
is `S_:934–953`; everything before 786 is the shared engine of §3.
